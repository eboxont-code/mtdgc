# Market launch readiness checklist

**Purpose:** an internal, evidence-led checklist for reviewing one exact market and purchase journey before it is offered to customers.

> **Template only.** Completing this document does not approve a country, seller, payment route, product, supplier, tax treatment or legal position. Every applicable gate starts as **NOT ASSESSED**. Do not publish a market claim or enable a checkout route from an incomplete checklist.

Complete one copy for each market, journey and legal seller arrangement. Do not reuse an approval across different entities, payment routes, products or countries.

## 2. Keep the three intended journeys separate

The Master Plan defines three distinct journeys. These are planning scopes, not proof that any route is currently approved or available.

### A. Nepal domestic

- [ ] Buyer and payer eligibility for Nepal is documented.
- [ ] NPR customer-facing pricing and the exact amount due are confirmed from current approved prices.
- [ ] The payment provider, receiving entity, settlement account, settlement currency and reconciliation method are verified.
- [ ] The delivery country, recipient details and redemption region are recorded for each exact product.
- [ ] Local seller, tax, consumer, refund, privacy and support requirements have been reviewed by qualified people.
- [ ] The complete Nepal domestic journey passes an authorised end-to-end test.

### B. Australia-local

- [ ] Australian buyer and payer eligibility is documented for the exact offer.
- [ ] AUD customer-facing pricing and the exact amount due are confirmed; the actual processor settlement currency is separately verified.
- [ ] The legal seller, merchant-of-record role, payment processor eligibility and receiving account are documented.
- [ ] Each SKU is independently confirmed as valid for the intended Australian customer, delivery country and redemption region.
- [ ] Australian tax, consumer, privacy, refund, complaint and disclosure requirements have qualified review.
- [ ] Local fulfilment capacity, support coverage and the complete Australia-local test journey are evidenced.
- [ ] The route remains disabled until the owner approves this exact market and scope.

### C. Australia buyer to Nepal recipient

- [ ] The Australian buyer/payer and Nepal recipient are represented as separate people and countries where applicable.
- [ ] The intended payer currency (the Master Plan's design target is AUD) is distinct from processor settlement currency and product denomination / face-value currency.
- [ ] The Nepal delivery destination, recipient contact and exact product redemption region are verified.
- [ ] The offer is a specifically identified digital product with documented supplier authority and fulfilment; it is not described or operated as remittance, cash-out, stored value or a wallet.
- [ ] The legal seller, merchant-of-record, payment, tax, consumer, refund, privacy and cross-border data responsibilities have qualified review for both relevant jurisdictions.
- [ ] The recipient hand-off, wrong-recipient process, failed delivery, refund route and customer support ownership are documented.
- [ ] The complete payer-to-recipient journey passes an authorised end-to-end test.
- [ ] The route remains disabled until the owner approves this exact journey and scope.

Do not silently send an Australian payer through an NPR-only domestic checkout. Do not infer buyer, payer, recipient, delivery or redemption country from IP address, device location, card country, email domain or product title.

## 3. Currency, price and fees

Record each amount separately; one currency field must never stand in for another.

- [ ] Customer display currency is documented.
- [ ] Checkout / amount-to-pay currency is documented.
- [ ] Payment processor settlement currency and receiving-account country are evidenced.
- [ ] Supplier quote currency and the exact quote source are evidenced.
- [ ] Code / gift-card face-value or product-denomination currency is recorded separately.
- [ ] Any FX source, rate, timestamp, conversion direction and revalidation / expiry rule are recorded.
- [ ] Payment, settlement, conversion, supplier, refund and delivery fees are sourced and included in the final payable amount where applicable.
- [ ] Rounding, minimum order, price-lock and unpaid-order expiry rules are approved and documented.
- [ ] The contribution calculation uses verified supplier cost, payment costs, FX, refund / fraud provision and variable fulfilment / support costs.
- [ ] The owner-approved margin floor and its measurement basis are recorded before any price is approved.
- [ ] Customer receipts and refund amounts use the correct currency and clearly distinguish face value from MTDGC selling price.

Never invent a rate, fee, tax, supplier cost, margin or selling price. Do not change a live price from an estimate, an expired quote or an unverified conversion.

## 4. Payment provider and settlement readiness

- [ ] The provider's current written terms permit the actual seller, countries, digital products, game top-ups, subscriptions, code delivery and customer journey.
- [ ] The legal account holder, receiving entity, settlement account country and permitted settlement currencies are verified.
- [ ] Fees, payout timing, reserves, chargebacks, disputes, failed payments and refund handling are documented from provider evidence.
- [ ] Payment instructions, amount, reference requirements and expiry behaviour match the approved route.
- [ ] Settlement is independently matched to the correct order and amount before fulfilment; a customer screenshot or submitted reference alone is not settlement confirmation.
- [ ] The reconciliation owner and exception process for underpayment, overpayment, wrong reference and unidentified transfers are assigned.
- [ ] A real end-to-end payment test has been authorised and completed for this exact route.

Do not advertise card, instant or automated payment until the relevant live end-to-end test succeeds. Do not change production payment settings as part of completing this checklist.

## 5. Seller, tax, consumer and policy review

- [ ] The legal seller name, trading name, registration jurisdiction and invoice issuer are verified.
- [ ] The merchant-of-record role and responsibility for payment, tax, fulfilment, refunds, complaints and customer data are documented.
- [ ] Relevant local business registration and licensing requirements have been reviewed by a qualified professional.
- [ ] Tax treatment for the actual seller, supply, product, customer market and cross-border route has qualified review.
- [ ] Terms, privacy, delivery, cancellation, refund and complaint wording accurately cover the exact market and journey.
- [ ] Non-excludable consumer rights are preserved; blanket wording is not treated as overriding applicable law.
- [ ] Required product, region, denomination, delivery, price and fee disclosures appear before the customer commits.
- [ ] Any legal or regulatory question has a dated written review from a qualified professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

This checklist is not legal or tax advice and makes no compliance determination. Do not invent statutory rights, tax rates, registration status, licence requirements or legal conclusions.

## 6. Supplier and product readiness

- [ ] Each supplier's legal entity and official source are independently verified.
- [ ] Written evidence supports supply origin and resale / distribution authority for the exact product, region, channel and denomination.
- [ ] The exact SKU, platform, account type, redemption region, restrictions and denomination are confirmed from an authoritative source.
- [ ] Current availability, supplier price, quote currency, quote date and expiry are recorded.
- [ ] Delivery method, realistic time window, cut-off, failure process and escalation contact are documented.
- [ ] Supplier terms permit the intended customer data, marketing, payment and support model.
- [ ] A separate product-level launch review and successful test order exist for each exact product / variant / region.
- [ ] No supplier workflow requires customer passwords, OTPs, 2FA codes, payment PINs, recovery codes, session tokens or unsafe account access.

Use the Supplier Register for supplier and product-scope evidence. Its current empty / unverified records are not approvals. A market checklist never substitutes for product-level supplier, region, price, availability, fulfilment and test-order evidence.

## 7. Fulfilment and customer support

- [ ] The manual or automated fulfilment steps are documented for each product family and destination.
- [ ] Staff capacity, operating days, time zone, cut-off and exception workload support the stated delivery window.
- [ ] Support languages, staffed hours and response commitments are explicitly approved and can actually be maintained.
- [ ] A named owner handles delayed, failed, incorrect, unavailable and duplicate delivery cases.
- [ ] Refund, cancellation, wrong-recipient, supplier-outage and escalation procedures are documented.
- [ ] Customers receive a truthful next step and update route without unsupported delivery or response-time promises.
- [ ] Support records link the order, payment, supplier action and customer conversation without relying on private chat alone.

Do not claim 24/7 support, instant delivery, guaranteed stock or a response-time promise without measured evidence and an approved operating commitment.

## 8. Privacy and cross-border data handling

- [ ] Every customer field collected for the journey has a stated purpose and necessity.
- [ ] The seller, payment provider, supplier, support provider and other recipients of personal data are identified.
- [ ] Each processor's country of access / storage and the relevant cross-border transfer basis have qualified review.
- [ ] Access is limited to staff who need the data for payment, fulfilment or support.
- [ ] Retention, deletion, correction and access-request handling are documented and approved.
- [ ] Payment proof and delivered codes remain in private, access-controlled storage.
- [ ] Customer passwords, OTPs, payment PINs, recovery codes and unnecessary account access are never requested or stored.

Do not place personal data, customer evidence, private supplier documents or secrets in Git. Link only to an approved private evidence reference.

## 9. Checkout, operations and technical readiness

- [ ] Buyer country, billing / payer country, recipient country, delivery country and redemption region are separate values where relevant.
- [ ] Payer currency, checkout amount, processor settlement currency, product face-value currency and supplier quote currency are shown or recorded distinctly.
- [ ] Product-specific customer details are validated before charging, with a final confirmation for irreversible top-ups or code releases.
- [ ] The order, payment, fulfilment and refund records retain the exact approved journey and product scope.
- [ ] Duplicate submission, retry, payment mismatch, supplier failure and partial fulfilment behaviour are tested safely.
- [ ] Customer order tracking, receipts, support links and policy links work for the exact journey.
- [ ] Security, access control, storage and audit requirements pass the existing repository quality checks.
- [ ] Any required database change is separately reviewed against the approved Supabase cutover and migration gates; this checklist does not authorise a migration.

## 10. Controlled end-to-end test

- [ ] The owner has approved the test account, exact product / variant, market route and test method.
- [ ] Any purchase, payment fee or other possible charge has explicit owner approval before the test begins.
- [ ] The test confirms the buyer, payer, recipient, delivery country and redemption region are correct.
- [ ] The selected product, variant, denomination, currencies, fees and final payable amount remain consistent through checkout and receipt.
- [ ] Payment settlement is independently matched to the correct order and amount.
- [ ] The exact supplier fulfilment completes and the delivered item matches the test scope.
- [ ] Tracking, customer instructions, support and failure / refund handling are checked.
- [ ] The date, reviewer, scope and private evidence reference are recorded without customer data or secrets.

A preview, mock response, screenshot alone, incomplete transaction or another product's test is not a passed market test. Never use a real customer's order as test data. This checklist does not authorise a purchase or test order.

| Fulfilment and customer support | | | | | NOT ASSESSED |
| Privacy and cross-border data | | | | | NOT ASSESSED |

| Authorised end-to-end test | | | | | NOT ASSESSED |

Use only these overall states:

- **NOT ASSESSED** — review has not started or evidence has not been supplied.
- **HOLD** — a required item is missing, expired, inconsistent, unsafe or unresolved.
- **READY FOR OWNER REVIEW** — all applicable evidence and qualified reviews are complete and the authorised end-to-end test has passed.
- **APPROVED** — the owner has explicitly approved this exact seller, market, journey, payment route and product scope.
- **REJECTED** — the exact scope fails a required legal, provider, supplier, product, safety or evidence gate.

READY FOR OWNER REVIEW is not approval. Only the owner may move an exact scope to APPROVED. Any required item that is NOT ASSESSED or on HOLD keeps the route disabled and must not be described as supported. A checklist entry does not change a product record, price, availability, checkout, database, payment setting or public market claim.

## 12. Revalidation and change control

Reopen the checklist whenever the seller, country, payer / recipient route, payment provider, settlement account, currency, tax treatment, supplier, SKU, redemption region, price, fees, delivery process, support coverage, policy or data processor changes. Revalidate all time-limited evidence before its expiry and after a material incident. Preserve prior decisions and record a dated replacement; do not erase a rejected or suspended history.
